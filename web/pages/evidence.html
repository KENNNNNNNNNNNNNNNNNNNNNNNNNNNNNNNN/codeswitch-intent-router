    Limits of this evidence

    • The labelled data is 120 short, synthetic registrar messages, not real student writing.
    • The model made only 5 mistakes on them, too few to show whether mixing languages lowers accuracy.
    • Most mistakes come from two overlapping categories (student records vs document requests), not from code-switching.
    • Only the campus-registrar categories were tested. Other category lists and the translations are untested.
    • Confidence is the number the model states about itself, not a measured probability.

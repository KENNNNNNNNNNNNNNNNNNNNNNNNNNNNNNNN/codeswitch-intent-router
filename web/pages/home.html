Read Taglish the way people actually write it.

Taglish Router follows a message word by word, marks where it switches between Tagalog and English, works out what the sender wants, and can rewrite the whole thing in one language.

Try a message How it works See the evidence

Who it's for

Anyone who receives messages in mixed Tagalog and English: a registrar's office, a support desk, a barangay health centre, or a person who just wants a message in one language. You choose the categories it sorts messages into.

Why it was built

Filipinos switch languages mid-sentence all the time, and AI models answer such messages fluently and confidently even when they understand them less. This project measures how mixed a message is, and hands it to a person when the model isn't sure.
